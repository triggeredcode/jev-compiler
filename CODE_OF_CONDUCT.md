# Code of conduct

Jev Compiler is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's credentials, private policies, recordings, or data.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Correct mistakes openly and help keep project discussions focused.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban people
whose behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/triggeredcode/jev-compiler/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, release channels, and
public events where someone represents the project.
